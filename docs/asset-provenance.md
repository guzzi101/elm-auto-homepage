## September 26 plate retouch

The Family and Adventure stock photos were edited with the built-in image generation tool to remove their front registration plates. The prompt requested only plate/holder removal and reconstruction of the original bumper/grille, preserving the vehicle, camera framing and landscape. The final web assets are `dist/assets/stock-family.webp` and `dist/assets/stock-adventure.webp`. Work and Commute have no visible front registration plates. Customer handover photos remain the original Elm photographs.
